# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates in the project's
spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project
channel) is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every
background and level of experience, accept criticism of your work
gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and do not harass,
insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer,
Dmitrij Koniajev ([@dimchansky](https://github.com/dimchansky) on GitHub).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer decides on the
consequences following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban,
permanent ban).
